BANYAN MUTUAL (fictional)
Policy summary for Individual Hospital Cash and Cover

--- Page 1 ---
This policy gives you hospital cover up to a sum insured of Rupees Four Lakh for each policy year. We will pay hospital room rent up to Rupees Three Thousand Five Hundred for each day you stay in hospital, and if you are admitted to an intensive care unit we will pay up to Rupees Seven Thousand for each day. You will have to bear ten percent of every claim yourself as co-payment. A deductible of Rupees Five Thousand applies to each claim.

--- Page 2 ---
We will not pay for any illness that starts within thirty (30) days of the policy start date. For conditions you already had when you bought the policy, cover begins once you have completed forty-eight months with us. Hernia, cataract and piles are covered only after you have completed two years. For cataract surgery, our maximum payment is Rupees Thirty-Five Thousand for each eye. For appendix removal, our maximum payment is Rupees Fifty Thousand.

--- Page 3 ---
This policy does not cover: cosmetic surgery, treatment for infertility, dental treatment, and treatment for sexually transmitted infections other than HIV. We do not restrict your choice of hospital.

SAPPHIRE LIFE & HEALTH (fictional)
Customer Information Sheet: Senior Care Plan (age 60 and above)

--- Page 1 ---
What is covered: in-patient hospitalisation, day-care procedures, domiciliary treatment.
Sum insured: Rs. 7,50,000 (seven lakh fifty thousand) for each policy year.
Room rent: We pay room rent up to 2% of the sum insured per day.
Intensive care is payable at actual cost; there is no separate ICU limit.
Co-payment: A compulsory co-payment of 20% applies to every claim. This is not optional.
There is no deductible in this plan.

--- Page 2 ---
Waiting periods
Any illness arising in the first 30 days after the start of the policy is not covered, except accidental injury.
Diseases that you had before buying the policy are covered after 2 years of continuous renewal.
Cataract, glaucoma and knee replacement are covered after 2 years.
There are no sub-limits on any individual procedure.

--- Page 3 ---
We do not pay for:
(a) cosmetic surgery
(b) treatment taken outside India
(c) hormone replacement therapy
(d) investigation and evaluation only, without treatment
(e) injury caused by participation in hazardous sports
Claims are payable at any hospital; no network restriction applies.

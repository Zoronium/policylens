LAKEVIEW MUTUAL HEALTH (fictional)
CUSTOMER INFORMATION SHEET - Family Plus Floater

--- Page 1 ---
Section A. Cover
Sum Insured: Rs. 10 lakh on a family floater basis.
Room rent: Up to Rs. 6,000 per day for a single private room. No proportionate deduction is applied to pharmacy or diagnostics.
ICU / ICCU: Up to Rs. 12,000 per day.
Co-payment: 20% co-payment applies to claims for insured persons aged 60 years and above, and to all claims at non-network hospitals.
Deductible: Rs. 25,000 per claim. The insurer pays only the amount above the deductible.

--- Page 2 ---
Section B. Waiting periods
Initial waiting period: 15 days.
Pre-existing conditions are covered after 24 months.
Specific waiting periods: hysterectomy 24 months; kidney stone 24 months; hernia 12 months.
Section C. Limits on specific treatments
Cataract: Rs. 35,000 per eye. Maternity-related complications: Rs. 75,000.

--- Page 3 ---
Section D. Not covered
1. Cosmetic surgery
2. Self-inflicted injury
3. Adventure sports injuries
4. Hearing aids and spectacles
5. Experimental treatment
Section E. Hospitals
Cover applies at any hospital registered in India. A network hospital list is available on request.
